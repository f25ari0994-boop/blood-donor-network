ABOUT THE PROJECT

Bringing blood donors and people in need together

A coordinated digital network for donor discovery, emergency blood requests, controlled matching, direct communication and donation receipt tracking.

01

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify the process of finding potential blood donors and coordinating emergency blood requests. We aim to provide a structured alternative to scattered phone calls and social media messages.

02

Our Vision

We envision a connected community where donors and people seeking blood can find relevant information and coordinate assistance through a reliable and accessible platform.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple and organized process

The platform brings important blood donation coordination activities together.

01

Create Your Account

Register using your personal information and securely access your account. You can register as a donor or participate as a requester.

02

Explore or Request Blood

Explore compatible blood groups, register as a donor, or create an emergency request with the required blood units and hospital information.

03

Match, Donate and Confirm

Donors accept requests, requesters confirm matches, and both parties track donation and receipt progress.

How the complete matching process works

  1. Request: A requester creates a blood request and specifies the required blood group and units.
  2. Discovery: The platform identifies potentially compatible, available and eligible donors.
  3. Acceptance: A donor accepts a request. A pending acceptance reserves that donor from accepting another request.
  4. Confirmation: The requester confirms or rejects the proposed match. A rejected match releases the donor.
  5. Communication: Once confirmed, the matched parties can contact each other using Call and WhatsApp actions.
  6. Donation: The donor reports the donation and the requester confirms the actual units received.
  7. Completion: Confirmed received units reduce the remaining requirement. Once all requested units are received, the request is marked fulfilled and the donor becomes available for another match.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Safe and responsible blood donation

Medical compatibility and eligibility

Blood group compatibility displayed by this platform is intended for preliminary donor discovery only. Actual transfusion suitability must always be determined by qualified medical professionals through appropriate blood-bank testing. Donor health and eligibility must also be assessed by qualified medical staff. This platform does not replace medical advice, clinical screening or blood-bank procedures.
