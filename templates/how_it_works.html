How It Works

  1. Create an account and register your donor profile, or submit a blood request.
  2. Eligible donors can review compatible requests and send an acceptance.
  3. The requester reviews and confirms or rejects the proposed match.
  4. After confirmation, the matched parties can coordinate directly.
  5. The donor reports the donation and the requester confirms the units received.

Donor eligibility and health screening rules apply. The platform does not replace professional medical advice.
