Before You Register

A few important things to remember.

Correct Blood Group

Select your blood group carefully. Medical professionals must confirm blood compatibility before transfusion.

Accurate Location

Provide your city or area so potential recipients can identify donors in a suitable location.

90-Day Donation Interval

The system uses your last donation date to apply its minimum 90-day interval rule.

Private Health Information

Health answers should be restricted to authorised handling and never shown in public donor search results.

Important: Registration does not mean you are medically approved to donate. A qualified healthcare professional must assess your suitability before donation. The system's 90-day rule is an administrative screening rule, not a complete medical eligibility assessment.
